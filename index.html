About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center (YMSC), Tsinghua University, working with Prof. Jie Ma. I work in spectral graph theory and extremal combinatorics.

I study how the eigenvalues of a graph control its structure. With my collaborators, I have obtained a series of results on the spectral radius and the number of substructures in a graph. These results strengthen and extend several classical theorems in extremal graph theory, such as the Erdős–Rademacher theorem, the Erdős–Faudree–Rousseau theorem, the Lovász–Simonovits theorem, the Mubayi theorem and the Pikhurko–Yilma theorem. Another part of my work concerns spectral extremal problems with a given number of edges. Using the probabilistic method, we determined the order of magnitude of the booksize of Nosal graphs and proved an asymptotically sharp counting theorem for 4-cycles. Moreover, we established an asymptotic formula and a stability theorem for an arbitrary non-bipartite forbidden graph F. These two results unify the theorems of Erdős, Stone and Simonovits on Turán numbers and those of Nikiforov on the vertex-spectral version. By developing an edge-spectral stability method, we also solved the edge-spectral extremal problem for color-critical graphs with chromatic number at least 4. For almost-bipartite graphs, we obtained an asymptotic formula and the structure of the extremal graphs.

More recently, my collaborators and I proved that Sidorenko’s conjecture is equivalent to its spectral strengthening. As an application, we obtained asymptotically sharp lower bounds on the number of copies of complete bipartite graphs and even cycles in graphs whose spectral radius exceeds that of the corresponding split graph with the same number of edges. I also used the entropy method to build a bridge from weighted Turán theorems to spectral ones. For every color-critical graph F, this method gives sharp bounds on the spectral radius of F-free graphs in terms of walk counts. When F has chromatic number at least 4, these bounds include both the vertex-spectral and the edge-spectral Turán theorems. Beyond spectral graph problems, I also study intersecting and cross-intersecting families in extremal set theory.

I am currently supported by the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation (No. 2026M793384), the Postdoctoral Fellowship Program of the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation (No. GZC20261656) and the National Natural Science Foundation of China (No. 12601675).
